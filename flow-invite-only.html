Polysocial in Claude · campaign flow

Invite-only campaign, designed by the brand

The brand writes the brief itself and chooses invite only, so the campaign never appears in the open creator feed. Claude shortlists matching creators, the brand picks who gets an invite, and only those creators can see and join it.

Tool calls on the invite-only path

set_campaign_accessnewSets access: "invite_only" on the draft. The campaign is hidden from the creator feed and only invited creators can open it.
match_creatorsnewRanks creators against the brief: audience, city, platforms, category and past results. Claude shows the top matches in the chat.
set_invite_listnewSaves the creators the brand picked, plus any added by @handle, and a list of backups.
publish_campaignchangedNeeds the brand's approval. Holds the budget and sends invites to the list instead of notifying every matching creator.
get_invite_statusnewCounts accepted, declined, pending and expired invites, so Claude can suggest inviting backups.

Decision points

  1. Brand connected? If not, the manager signs in to Polysocial and Claude checks again.
  2. Hire a strategist? Yes goes to the strategist path (#strat). No keeps the brand as the designer.
  3. Brief complete? check_brief flags gaps and Claude asks for them before drafting.
  4. Who can join? Open to all publishes as today. Invite only continues down this flow.
  5. Accept invite? Each creator accepts, declines, or lets the invite expire in their Invites tab.
  6. Enough joined? If not, Claude offers to invite the backups from the shortlist.

Tool names are a proposal for the Polysocial MCP server, not a published API. The invite card matches what creators already see for strategist invites in the collaborator app.
